# Verification gaps — what still needs a source
Generated by `scripts/verify_genes.py` against NCBI Gene and PubMed. Each item below is a *specific* missing thing, not a general doubt.

**What counts as resolved:** a gene symbol that matches an NCBI Gene record for the stated species, and a reference whose author+year+gene finds the paper in PubMed. A PMID found only by topic search is listed as unconfirmed, because it may be a different paper about the same gene.

## Read this first — three caveats

**1. Some listed 'genes' are not gene symbols.** The draft wrote descriptive phrases inside the gene field, and the parser took them as symbols. These need no NCBI record because they are not genes: `uORF` (a sequence feature of LsGGP2), `ROS scavengers`, `ABA catabolism`, `LOG-related prickle loci`, `for gynoecy`. Do not go looking for them.

**2. Some are two names for one gene.** `PLETHORA`/`PLT`, `HAG1`/`MYB28`, `AT3`/`Pun1`, and `Bt`/`CsBt`, `Bl`/`CsBl`, `Bi`/`CsBi` (the Cs prefix is the species tag, not a different locus). Counting them separately understates the resolution rate.

**3. The three GAP-* entries are Level 5 hypotheses by design.** They name gene *families* and networks (SOS1/2, NHX, HKT, DREB, HSP, WOX, LBD), not specific loci, so 'no NCBI Gene record' is the expected result and not a defect. What they need is not a gene ID but a decision: either pin each family to a named crop ortholog, or keep them as research questions and stop presenting them as atlas entries.

**What would actually help:** for each item below, a DOI or PMID of a paper that functionally tests that gene in that crop. Gene symbol + species + DOI is enough; the pipeline resolves the rest. Please do not supply reconstructed citations — an author/year without a findable DOI is what the current draft already has.
