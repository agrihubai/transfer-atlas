Proof-of-concept seed. A few curated, functionally-validated entries — not a complete atlas. Gene function, source species and evidence are sourced (links on each card). The portability tag, the target-crop note and the IP flag are indicative research signals, not lab results or legal advice: an IP flag means patent/application activity appears to touch this gene or use and freedom-to-operate must be checked, not that a given use is barred. Every candidate still needs allele checking and validation in the target crop. Patent status is meant to be filled automatically from open patent data (PatentsView / Lens).
