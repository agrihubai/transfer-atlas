How this atlas is built, and what is actually verified

Current state

Known limitation

Genome-project locus tags (Solyc, Csa, Cla97, MELO3C, SMEL4.1, BraA) are not indexed in NCBI, so most of them could not be checked there. A missing confirmation is not evidence that an ID is wrong — it means NCBI is the wrong database to ask. Resolving them needs Ensembl Plants or Sol Genomics.

How the readiness label is decided

Gene identity and references are machine-verified against NCBI Gene and PubMed by scripts/verify_genes.py, which records only what the databases return.

Route, linkage drag, side effects and patent signals are indicative research signals curated by a breeder — not lab results and not legal classifications. Every candidate still needs allele checking in your own material and validation in the target crop.
